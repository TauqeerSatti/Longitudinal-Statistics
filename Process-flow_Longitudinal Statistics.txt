
Statistical Analysis using FreeSurfer Longitudnal Two stage model


Stage 1: The first stage reduces the temporal data within each subject to a single statistic (e.g. annualized atrophy rate or percent change)

#for the left and right hemispheres considering cortical thickness and volume  measures
# qdec long.qdec.table.dat contains fsid	fsid-base	years	CNR	Tesla values for each patient pre and post operation

long_mris_slopes  --sd /MRI-Patient-Data-Location --qdec long.qdec.table.dat --meas thickness volume --hemi rh --do-avg --do-rate --do-pc1 --do-spc --do-stack --do-label --time years --qcache fsaverage
